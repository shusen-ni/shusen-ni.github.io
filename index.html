Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Zedong Zheng. My research focuses on high-performance control and fault-tolerant control of multiphase motor drives, with particular interests in fundamental–harmonic current decoupling based on generalized symmetrical components and virtual phase shifting, medium-voltage DC propulsion topologies and coordinated control, hybrid SiC/Si traction inverters, and intelligent fault-tolerant control. My work integrates control theory, power-electronic topology design, condition monitoring, and experimental validation to develop reliable high-power electric-drive systems.

I received my B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Shandong University in 2022. I ranked 1st among 241 students for three consecutive years and was recommended for direct Ph.D. admission to Tsinghua University.
